We’re excited to announce a powerful new feature in DUOS: the ability to create your own branded and curated Data Library! Now, organizations and research groups can showcase their datasets with a customized look and feel—tailored to reflect their unique identity and mission.

With this feature, you can:

-   Design a branded landing page for your data collections
-   Curate datasets to highlight key themes or research areas
-   Guide users directly to data that matters most to your community

Whether you’re amplifying your research impact or simplifying access to your data resources, this new capability makes it easier than ever to connect researchers with the data they need.

Check out some examples on the [DUOS homepage](https://duos.org), and if you’re interested in creating a branded Data Library in DUOS to showcase your research and share your data contact us via the homepage to let us know!
